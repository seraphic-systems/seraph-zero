Give organizational structure a limited scope. It can coordinate work and maintain continuity without turning the entire person into an optimization project.

Protect room for individual creativity, relationships, and unmodeled life. An organization may need an explicit system where a person already has a mind; that distinction should constrain what the system attempts to manage.
