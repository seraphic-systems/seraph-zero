Some goods can be detached from their makers and exchanged: a plugin, a recording, a finished artifact. Practice, formation, friendship, and mastery have to be lived.

Sell the transferable goods honestly and transmit the practice through participation. Let a product support lived goods without pretending that purchasing it transfers those goods as well.
