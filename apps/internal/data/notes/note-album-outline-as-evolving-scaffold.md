An album outline can hold several possible sequences of questions, stages, and track pairings. It gives the work enough shape to proceed while leaving room for the music to change the arrangement.

Keep variants where a choice remains unresolved. A scaffold is useful when it supports making rather than turning a provisional sequence into an obligation.
