Some human goods require contact with something outside one’s control: another person who can answer independently, a world that can resist, an effort whose outcome is uncertain.

An experience can be convincing in appearance while missing this reciprocity. What matters includes whether it can surprise us, ask something of us, or change the terms of the encounter.
