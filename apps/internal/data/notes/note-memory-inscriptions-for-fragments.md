Capture memories beside musical fragments while recall is available. Context, feeling, and recollection can give a fragment an inscription that technical metadata cannot supply.

Preserve uncertainty in those memories. A recollection can enrich an archive without becoming an unsupported claim about the fragment’s provenance.
