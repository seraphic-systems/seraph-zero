A weekly release can connect making, explanation, and sharing. Major changes receive an essay explaining their meaning; smaller changes receive concise notes. Distribution becomes part of the practice rather than an activity deferred until everything is finished.

The rhythm still needs room for unfinished work. A reliable opportunity to share should not force every branch to be complete.
