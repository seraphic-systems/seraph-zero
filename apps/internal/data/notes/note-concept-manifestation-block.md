A concept is the meaning being explored. A manifestation is a particular realization: a draft, demo, mix, or rewrite. Blocks are the smaller expressive components from which manifestations are made.

This model lets a concept change without confusing it with a file. A manifestation that changes enough in meaning may become a different concept.
