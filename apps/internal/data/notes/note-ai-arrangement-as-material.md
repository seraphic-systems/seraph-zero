A possible arrangement workflow: establish the desired structure in Ableton, generate arrangement ideas, refine selected material, add vocals, and return to Ableton for final decisions.

Generation expands the possibilities available for editing. Responsibility for what survives into the finished arrangement remains with the process of selection and revision.
