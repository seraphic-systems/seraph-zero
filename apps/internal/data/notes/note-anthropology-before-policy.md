Policy operates within assumptions about what a person is and what a life is for. Those assumptions shape what people can imagine, desire, and recognize as improvement.

The proposed chain—politics, culture, anthropology, metaphysics, theology—helps expose deeper commitments beneath a practical disagreement, even where influence also runs in other directions.
