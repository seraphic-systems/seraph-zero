Give dense utilities a place of their own within an imagined world. A device or liminal layer can hold apps and controls while other environments keep their particular atmosphere.

Hardware, simulated space, and graphic surfaces can form distinct layers. Some tools may appear only when needed, rather than remaining as a permanent overlay.
