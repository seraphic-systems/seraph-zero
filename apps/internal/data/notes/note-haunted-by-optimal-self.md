The imagined optimal person can become an alien figure: perfectly adapted, coherent, and complete, but estranged from the flaws of actual human life. Pursuing that image may produce its own haunting.

One possible reading of the alien sound and failed utopia is the distance between this manufactured ideal and the humanity still trying to arrive through it.
