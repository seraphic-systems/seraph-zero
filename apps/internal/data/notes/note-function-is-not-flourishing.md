A person can be adapted to an environment and still lose capacities that matter. Measures of engagement or low friction may miss the difference between peace and an attention shaped entirely by the feed.

Flourishing also asks about sustained attention, solitude, unperformed presence, and an inner life that is not always awaiting capture as content.
