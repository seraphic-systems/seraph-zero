A shared creative world could grow through human-scale communities joined by a common orientation and creative language. Local aesthetic differences could develop without losing that shared foundation.

Beauty and communion would be purposes of coordination. The early sketch proposed communities of roughly 150 people; the more lasting question is what scale allows people to recognize one another and sustain a shared practice.
