Different aspects of a creative world answer different questions:

- Principles: what is believed.
- Aesthetic: what it feels like.
- Ontology: what kinds of things exist.
- Practices: how people make things.
- Architecture: how those things relate.
- Governance: how decisions happen.
- Interfaces: how people participate.

Naming the aspect helps locate what a disagreement is actually about.
