Deliberate structure can protect the conditions for human experience inside systems too large for an individual to comprehend. Tools can support continuity, orientation, and care when the surrounding environment does not.

The limit is whether the structure preserves human possibilities or begins to replace them. How much structure belongs around a person remains an open tension.
