## Current focus

Direct work on the album is currently paused while developing an initial
creative community and the world and aesthetic around the work. Additional
community is a prerequisite for the shared undertaking envisioned here.

The immediate focus is [Establish the Creative Foundation](../horizons/establish-the-creative-foundation.md),
through [publishing writing on Zero](publish-writing-on-zero.md) and
[releasing short audiovisual works](release-short-audiovisual-works.md).
These practices have value in themselves while building the conditions for
returning to the album together.

## Rationale

- **Creation:** Give form to lived experience through music and the digital world around it.
- **Communion:** Build relationships through making something together.
- **Contemplation:** Invite others to encounter what we find beautiful and worth loving.
- **Sustainability:** Learn whether the work can help support continued creative practice. This is an intention to test, rather than an assumed result.
