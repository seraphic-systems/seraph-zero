Seraphic Systems Co.

A creative technology studio designing organizations as works of art.

Why

Humans are surrounded by large-scale systems built to capture attention and feed on desire. When left to drift, we often fall toward them, like matter toward a black hole.

It is possible to create something with a different center of gravity. Groups of people who orient toward one another to create things that they find meaningful. When matter comes together to resist falling, it can ignite like a star.

What a seraph is

A seraph is a human-scale organization conceived as a Gesamtkunstwerk, a total work of art. Each one aims to align sustainability through value creation, a physical space to gather and create, and a digital expression to enhance its capabilities and reach those who can't be there physically.

Built to stay itself

Seraphs measure themselves by whether they remain what they set out to be, choose every technology deliberately, and sell only what can be sold.

Seraph Zero

This project tells the story of an angel who reaches back through time to bring the first seraph into being. It unfolds over time and across space as the seraph is gradually drawn into reality.
