↓ A different center of gravity

Why

Left to drift, we tend to follow the nearest pull, like matter toward a black hole.

A different center of gravity is possible. People can turn toward one another, gathering around work they find meaningful. Under the right conditions, what gathers can become a source of light.

What is a seraph?

A seraph is a human-scale organization conceived as a Gesamtkunstwerk, a total work of art. Each brings together a way to sustain itself by creating value, a physical space to gather and create, and a digital presence that extends its capabilities and connects people beyond the space.

Seraph Zero

This project tells the story of an angel, a being outside of time, reaching into the world to bring the first seraph into reality. The story unfolds across time and space as the seraph gradually takes shape.

The future

Studios that inspire wonder and make room for people to gather and create. Digital worlds shaped by the communities they connect. In time, a network of seraphs that challenge and strengthen one another.
