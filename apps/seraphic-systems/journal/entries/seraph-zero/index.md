This project tells the story of an angel, a being outside of time, reaching into the world to bring the first seraph into reality. The story unfolds across time and space as the seraph gradually takes shape.

## A place to gather

A seraph is a human-scale organization conceived as a _Gesamtkunstwerk_, a total work of art. Each brings together a way to sustain itself by creating value, a physical space to gather and create, and a digital presence that extends its capabilities and connects people beyond the space.
